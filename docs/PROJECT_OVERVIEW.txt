SLOTERA — PROJECT OVERVIEW

Slotera is a lightweight client workspace for independent consultants, coaches,
instructors, and small expert-led studios. It brings bookings, session management,
client preparation, packages, and follow-up into one calm, focused workspace—without
turning into a heavyweight CRM or generic reservation system.

WHO IT IS FOR

Slotera is designed for service providers who run expert-led appointments, coaching,
consulting sessions, classes, and workshops. Its first product story centres on
independent consultants, coaches, and instructors, while keeping the underlying model
flexible for different service businesses.

WHAT IT HELPS WITH

- A customer booking page for browsing services, choosing a time, completing prep forms,
  and reviewing payment details.
- An operator workspace for managing services, availability, calendar sessions, bookings,
  clients, forms, packages, internal notes, and session action items.
- A scheduling experience with one-off and recurring sessions, workspace availability,
  blackout dates, and conflict prevention.
- Client context that stays lightweight: contact details, booking history, and private
  rich-text notes for session preparation and follow-up.
- Multi-session packages presented as ordered bundles of existing services.
- A separate superadmin workspace for platform staff to view safe workspace facts and
  provision new workspaces.
- English, Turkish, and German interface support for core navigation and labels.

PRODUCT APPROACH

Slotera is intentionally warm, simple, and service-led. It uses the language of bookings
and sessions, not reservations or a customer portal. The product is built to support paid
work, preparation, and follow-up while avoiding unnecessary CRM-style complexity.

CURRENT DEVELOPMENT STATUS

The public-facing experience is a polished Next.js prototype backed by deterministic mock
data, making it suitable for portfolio and client demonstrations. Payment forms, booking
confirmation, and customer management are therefore demonstrative in the default version;
they are not connected to a live payment provider, email service, or production customer
authentication system.

Alongside the prototype, Slotera has a local FastAPI and PostgreSQL backend in active
development. It already includes secure operator and superadmin sessions, tenant-aware
data isolation, services, client profiles, booking-ledger reads, forms, notes,
notifications, availability, recurring sessions, calendar-conflict enforcement, dashboard
facts, search, and limited superadmin workspace provisioning. The backend is opt-in for
local development and does not replace the public mock demo.

TECHNOLOGY

Frontend: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
Backend: FastAPI, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, Alembic
Developer experience: Docker Compose, OpenAPI-generated TypeScript transport types,
strict type checking, linting, and automated backend tests

WHAT COMES NEXT

The next phases focus on real public booking transactions, capacity-safe booking commands,
transactional confirmation email and booking-management links. Stripe payments, advanced
email automation, and external calendar integrations are planned later, after those core
flows are reliable.
