# Data Processing Agreement

> **Draft for legal review.** This text has not been reviewed by a solicitor and is not yet in force. Bracketed items must be completed before launch.

Last updated: [date of publication]

This agreement forms part of the [Terms of Service](/terms) between **Merulio Ltd** ("processor") and the operator using Slotera ("controller"), and meets Article 28 of the UK GDPR.

## 1. Scope

- **Subject matter:** hosting and processing the operator's client and booking data so the operator can take and manage bookings.
- **Duration:** for as long as the operator uses Slotera, plus the deletion period below.
- **Data subjects:** the operator's clients and prospective clients.
- **Personal data:** names, contact details, billing addresses, booking details, form answers, payment status and notes.
- **Special category data:** not intended. The controller must not use forms to collect it without its own safeguards.

## 2. Processor obligations

The processor will:

- process personal data only on the controller's documented instructions, which are given by using Slotera's features;
- ensure people with access are bound by confidentiality;
- keep appropriate technical and organisational security measures, including encryption in transit, tenant isolation in the database, and access limited to what each role needs;
- help the controller answer data-subject requests, including through the export and erasure tools on each client's page;
- notify the controller without undue delay, and within 48 hours where possible, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting their data;
- delete or return the controller's data when the service ends, except where the law requires retention;
- make available the information needed to show compliance with this agreement.

## 3. Sub-processors

The controller authorises these sub-processors:

- **Hetzner Online GmbH** — hosting and backups, EU.
- **Resend** — transactional email, [location].

The processor will give at least 30 days' notice of new sub-processors, during which the controller may object and end the service.

## 4. International transfers

Where data is transferred outside the UK, the processor relies on UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Addendum.

## 5. Liability

Liability under this agreement is subject to the limits in the Terms of Service.
